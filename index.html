What am I looking at?

The Smith chart plots the reflection coefficient Γ = (z−1)/(z+1) of a load impedance z = Z/Z0 (normalized to the reference impedance) inside the unit circle.

Solid blue circles are lines of constant resistance r. Dashed green arcs are lines of constant reactance x (above the real axis = inductive, below = capacitive).

The dotted circle centered on the chart is the constant-VSWR circle through your plotted point — every impedance on that circle produces the same standing-wave ratio on the line.
